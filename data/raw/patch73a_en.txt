Wild Rift Patch Notes 7.3a
Balance changes are here with champion and item adjustments, jungle and Turret Plating tuning, and updates to AAA ARAM.
Game Updates
Wild Rift Game Design Team
The stage is set, but we’re making a few adjustments to the lineup. This patch, we’re tuning some of our marksmen, bringing Hwei’s masterpiece back within the margins, and making adjustments across items, the jungle, and Turret Plating. We’re also following up on AAA ARAM with a round of changes based on your feedback. Let’s get into it!
Reminder: content will be released throughout the patch.

GAME MODE CHANGES
AAA ARAM
Since AAA ARAM launched, we've received a lot of detailed feedback about your experiences with the mode. Thank you to everyone who has shared the issues you've encountered and your thoughts with us. With this hotfix, we're addressing a few areas that have been affecting the experience, and we'll continue monitoring how these changes perform.
First up is the HEARTSTEEL set. Rhythm Hunter has performed quite differently depending on whether it's facing melee or ranged team comps, so we're adjusting its trigger range and Steel Token multiplier while also making its visual feedback clearer. When Infinite Encore is taken as your fifth Augment after you've already picked up other HEARTSTEEL cards in your first four slots, it still increases your Steel Token gain multiplier, but any remaining rerolls can no longer be used. That didn't feel great, so we're adding the corresponding number of stacks to compensate. If you're able to obtain a sixth Rune Card, those additional rerolls can still be used.
The updated Ethereal Weapon has opened up a number of interesting builds, and we're happy to see players experimenting with them. However, we also need to keep the experience of playing against it in mind, so we're adding a 1-second cooldown per individual ability against the same target. Blitzcrank can still continuously electrocute enemies for now; we'll continue monitoring how this adjustment plays out in practice.
We also went a little too far with some of our previous Augment nerfs. We're partially reverting those nerfs with this hotfix, though their overall power will remain lower than before.
The overall meta still needs some time to settle, so we're keeping champion adjustments relatively light this time around. We'll continue monitoring how these changes perform and listening closely to your feedback. Thanks for continuing to share your thoughts with us, and we hope you have fun!
