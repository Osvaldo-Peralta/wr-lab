Umbra Blades: Every 12 second(s), Nocturne's next attack is empowered to strike the target and nearby enemies for 70 physical damage (120% ) and restore 16 Health (16 () + 30% ) per enemy hit.

Except for the main target, Umbra Blades deals only 50% damage against minions and heals 50% less from them.

Umbra Blades can Critically Strike the main target. Attacks reduce Umbra Blades' cooldown by 1 second(s), increased to 3 second(s) against champions and monsters.

NOCTURNE Jungle Pathing

Jungle Pathing represents the choices Junglers make in regards to when and where to gank. These choices include how they involve farming camps and buffs between their ganks, anticipating counterganks, and looking for opportunities to invade. It involves understanding the different abilities the champions in the game have and how they interact, as well as the overall interactions between the two team compositions.

The Pro Nocturne Jungle Path features a full jungle clear with a Red Buff start. Unlock (Q) DUSKBRINGER for his first ability as it deals considerable amounts of damage and boosts Nocturne's attack damage as long as you stand on the dusk path. As you reach level two, unlock (W) SHROUD OF DARKNESS because it gives Nocturne a lot of bonus passive attack speed and path towards the Krugs camp. Once Krugs are dead, the Raptors camp is next and it grants you level three— unlocking (E) UNSPEAKABLE HORROR, his third skill. At this point the Nocturne is a force to be reckoned with and will win most duels. Pathing to the Blue Buff side now allows you to clear Murk Wolves, the Blue Sentinel and Gromp which grant you level four so upgrade (Q) DUSKBRINGER. Lastly, kill Rift Scuttler and then rotate into a gank or steal enemy jungler's camps in order to gain as much XP as possible.

This Nocturne jungle clear is currently one of the most popular options when starting from Red Buff and pathing toward Dragon Lane for early pressure. However, it’s also very effective when starting from the opposite side, offering early access to Rift Herald and mid lane control.
