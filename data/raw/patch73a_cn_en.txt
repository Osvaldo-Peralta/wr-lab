League of Legends: Wild Rift – Patch 7.3a Notes ( translated from Chinese)

Welcome to Patch 7.3a! In this update, we are making balance adjustments to select champions and items—supplementing underperforming champions with combat strength while bringing overly dominant choices back to reasonable levels. At the same time, we are moderately slowing down early-to-mid game jungle clear pacing and lowering resistance against continuous split-pushing and sieging. In addition, based on community feedback, we have adjusted several Augment cards and champions in ARAM (Rune ARAM) to further refine your gameplay experience.

Update Schedule: September 29, 09:30 – 12:00 Reminder: Content will be rolled out continuously during this update.

Champion Adjustments

Hwei (Nerfed)

Hwei is inherently designed to require a high degree of mastery, but his current upward performance has exceeded our expectations. Since his win rate will likely rise further as players master his kit, we are reducing his overall damage across the board.

Map & System Changes

Smite

Smite's clear amplification is overly dominant, especially in early-to-mid pathing. We are dialing back its base burn damage against monsters while preserving full scaling once items come online.

Burn Damage against Monsters: 30–198 per second (based on level) >>> 22–162 per second (based on level)

Nexus

Turret plating already serves as a strong safeguard against fast pushes, allowing us to roll back the previous health buff to the Nexus.

Max Health: 5500 >>> 4000

Turret Plating

The bonus defensive stats granted upon losing a plate were stalling sieges too drastically. We are tuning down the durability spike and duration to make sustained pushes more rewarding.

Resistances Gained upon Plate Loss: 30 Armor & Magic Resist >>> 20 Armor & Magic Resist

Resistance Buff Duration: 20s >>> 10s

Game Mode Adjustments: Rune ARAM

Thank you for your extensive feedback following the release of Rune ARAM—especially to our test server pioneers! This hotfix addresses several gameplay pain points, and we will continue monitoring balance.

For Heartsteel cards: Rhythmic Hunt had excessive variance against full-melee versus ranged comps, so we extended the trigger range, lowered stack scaling, and sharpened visual effects. Infinite Encore, when selected as a 5th card after accumulating four previous Heartsteel cards, offered leftover reroll charges that could no longer be spent; it now directly grants those unspent reroll stacks upfront.

For Mirage Blade, while it enabled creative builds, each distinct ability now has a 1-second cooldown per target to maintain healthy counterplay (Blitzcrank can still chain-electrocute). In addition, several cards that were over-nerfed on the test server have received partial rollbacks, though they remain weaker than their previous-patch baselines.
